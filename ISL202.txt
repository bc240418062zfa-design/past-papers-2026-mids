📝 *REVIEW #16*
👤 *Shared by:* 🙂💓
📅 *Date:* 02-08-2026 | ⏰ 12: PM

💬 "Parent-to-child thing? Answer: Good manners / Knowledge of manners / Good education. (The Prophet PBUH said: "There is no better gift for children than teaching of good manners"). Madina ka tameer hony se pehly naam? (Kuba ya Yathrib) Answer: Yathrib (or Yathrib / ﯾﺜﺮﺏ). Prophet SAW ny first masjid tameer kari uska area? (1050ms^2 ya 1500) Answer: 1050 square meters (for Masjid-e-Nabawi initially; later expanded to 1425 m²). Ayat ka tarjama / Day night / Existence of day and night / Laila tul wanahar meaning? Answer: "Lail" means Night and "Nahar" means Day. Their alternation and creation are signs of Allah's power. Migration Prophet SAW ny kaha ki? (Ghar-e-Hira / Thaur / Madinah) Answer: Madinah (Yathrib). During the journey, the Prophet (PBUH) stayed in Ghar-e-Thaur (Cave of Thaur) for 3 days. Allah k nazdeek sabse pehly / zaroori rights / Walden k haqoq? Answer: Rights of Parents (Walidain ke Huqooq). Science kisy kehty hain / Scientific knowledge? Answer: Knowledge based on Observation and Experimentation (Proust / Study of nature). Ayat translation / Words meaning: Al-Jibal: Mountains (ﻣﺮﺩ / Pahad) Al-Nisa: Women (ﻋﻮﺭﺍﺕ / Aurat) Walidaini: Parents (ﻭﺍﻟﺪﯾﻦ) Wal-Aqrabin: Relatives / Close kin (ﻗﺮﯾﺒﯽ / Relatives) Ayat: Aurat k naseeb mein pehly walden phr shohr hota / Inheritance share: Answer: Inheritance (Meeraas / Share in property). Surah An-Nisa: "Men have a share in what their parents and nearest kin leave behind, and women have a share..." Enter hoty hi kya krna chahiye? Answer: Salam karna chahiye (Greet with Salam). Salam krty hain to kya hota? / Enter hoty permission: Answer: Asking permission and greeting brings blessings and peace (Command to take permission before entering houses). Talaq recoverable / Types? Answer: Two times (Talaq-e-Raj'i - revocable divorce can be given up to two times). Arabic (decisive of Haq-Mahr)? Answer: Haq Mahr (Mahr). Divorce do to kya aapko nahi krna chahiye? (Any demand / Snatching / Take back) Answer: Take back / Snatching the gifts or Haq Mahr given to her. Both sea (Samandar) milty hain to kya hota? / Limit of expectation / Barrier: Answer: Barrier (Barzakh / ﺑﺮﺯﺥ) between two seas so they do not transgress. Cosmology / Astronomy science dan ka naam? Answer: Abu Al-Rayhan Al-Biruni (or Al-Khwarizmi / Falakiyat). Rainfall pani niche ata phr bukhar ban k uper jata, konsa cycle? Answer: Water Cycle (Aabi Chakra). Medicine k liye konsi branch use hoti? (Pharmacology / Synology) Answer: Pharmacology (Adwiyaat). Three open and off Islamic state / Department? Answer: Iqtisadiyat (Economy), Siyasat (Politics), Bait-ul-Mal (Wealth/Finance). Prophet SAW rest krny k liye garden mein gye (Taif journey): Answer: Garden of Utbah and Sheeba (Where slave Adas served Him grapes). "Rabbi Zidni Ilma" ka tarjama? Answer: "O my Lord, increase me in knowledge." Allah ny misaal di... tum mere qareeb ho (Hazrat Abu Bakr ya Hazrat Usman)? Answer: Hazrat Abu Bakr Siddique (R.A) (Companion in the Cave/Migration). Baby born hota to Qur'anic/Biology term (G se start hota)? Answer: Gestation / Ghencha / Gharb (Embryo/Development in womb mentioned in Surah Al-Alaq / Al-Mu'minun). Hajj k duran Prophet SAW se Makkah/Quraish k leader ki mulaqat (Peace talk): Answer: Suhail bin Amr (during Treaty of Hudaibiyah) or Urwa bin Masood. Yahudi battle mein shaheed / defeat huwy? Answer: Battle of Khyber (and Banu Quraiza after Ghazwa-e-Ahzab). Madina Makkah se kis side par hai? (North / West / East / South) Answer: North (Shumaal). Trigonometry / Mathematics kisny ijaad ki / promote ki? Answer: Muslim Scientists / Greeks (Al-Battani, Al-Khwarizmi). Economy kmany ka source / Islamic tax: Answer: Ushr, Zakat, Jizya, Kharaj. Land tax fasal par konsa hota hai? Answer: Ushr (Land/Agricultural tax). Kitab al-Nabatat (Book of Plants) kis scientist ne likhi? Answer: Abu Hanifa Dinawari (Al-Dinawari). Sood (Interest) ko Arabic mein kya kehty hain? Answer: Riba (ﺭﺑﺎ). Sood jo khata / types? Answer: Riba Al-Fadl / Riba Al-Nasi'ah. Battle of Mutah k duran Roman Army ki taudad? Answer: 200,000 (2 Lakh Roman soldiers). Taif mein kitny days rhy Prophet SAW? Answer: 10 days. Praying / Fasting / Time limit boundary ko kya kehty? Answer: Al-Meeqat (ﺍﻟﻤﯿﻘﺎﺕ) or Mawaqeet. Islam sikhny k liye kya zaroori hai? Answer: Quran o Sunnah (Quran and Sunnah). Ya Allah pak G sb bachon ko kmyab krna Kisi k sdky mujhe ... b kmyab krna Ameen summa Ameen 🤲🏼💓"
